1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Product Name

LETTFLIX (LoveWorld Entertainment Flix)

Product Vision

Lettflix is a next-generation digital television ecosystem that unifies live television, radio, podcasts, creators, and video-on-demand entertainment into a single subscription-based platform.

Unlike traditional streaming platforms that focus primarily on on-demand content, Lettflix bridges the gap between traditional broadcasting and modern streaming by giving equal importance to both live channels and on-demand content discovery.

The platform enables audiences to discover, consume, and engage with premium content while empowering broadcasters, ministries, media organizations, and creators to distribute, monetize, manage, and analyze their content within one unified ecosystem.

2. PRODUCT MISSION

To build the world's leading digital television ecosystem where broadcasters, ministries, creators, radio stations, and entertainment companies can distribute live and on-demand content while viewers access everything through one seamless subscription.
